ZapZap is a multiplayer rummy-style card game, a variant of Yaniv, played online by 3 to 8 players, with friends or against bots.

The goal: empty your hand faster than everyone else. When your hand is worth 5 points or less, call ZapZap! If nobody holds a hand as low as yours, you win the round. But if someone does as well or better, your ZapZap is counteracted and you take a heavy penalty.

HOW TO PLAY

On your turn, you play cards:
• a single card, or a pair, three or four of a kind;
• a run of at least three consecutive cards of the same suit;
• jokers stand in for any card in a pair or a run.

Then you draw a card: the face-down card from the deck, or one of the cards the previous player just played.

An ace is worth 1 point, cards 2 to 10 their face value, the jack 11, the queen 12 and the king 13. A joker counts 0 when you call ZapZap… but 25 points if it is still in your hand at the end of the round.

Above 100 points, you are out. When only two players remain, the game goes to Golden Score: one last deciding round. The last player standing wins.

PLAY WITH YOUR FRIENDS

• Create a public game, or a private one with an invite code to send to your friends.
• Pick the number of players and fill the free seats with bots.
• The other players' moves show up live, without reloading.

OR AGAINST BOTS

Not enough players? Fill the table with bots, from Easy to Hard, and start a game whenever you like.

A TABLE THAT READS ITSELF

• The plays your hand allows are suggested to you.
• Your hand's value stays on screen: you know when you can call ZapZap.
• At the end of every round, you see every player's hand and points.

HISTORY AND STATISTICS

• Find every game you finished, with the final standings and the detail of each round.
• Follow your statistics: games played, wins, average score, your ZapZap success rate.
• See how you rank against the other players on the global leaderboard.

YOUR ACCOUNT

Sign in with your Google account, or create an account with a username and a password. Your games, your history and your statistics follow you from the Android app to the browser.

ZapZap is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. An Internet connection is required to play.

The app is available in English, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, German, Russian, Japanese, Hindi, Indonesian and Arabic.
